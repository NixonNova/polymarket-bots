Thoughts
Hong Kong Time (HKT) is UTC+8 year-round (no daylight saving).
GFS Update Schedule (4 runs per day)
The GFS model initializes at these UTC times:
00z (00:00 UTC)
06z (06:00 UTC)
12z (12:00 UTC)
18z (18:00 UTC)

Data becomes available on sites like Tropical Tidbits, Windy, Pivotal Weather, etc., roughly 3.5–5.5 hours later (sometimes up to 6 hours).
Converted to Hong Kong Time (HKT = UTC+8):
GFS Run (Initialization)	Approx. Data Available (HKT)
00:00 UTC	08:00 – 10:30 HKT
06:00 UTC	14:00 – 16:30 HKT
12:00 UTC	20:00 – 22:30 HKT
18:00 UTC	02:00 – 04:30 HKT (next day)
Best time to check: 30–60 minutes after the “approx. available” window starts for each run.

ECMWF Update Schedule (Main runs)
ECMWF mainly runs at 00z and 12z (with shorter supplementary runs at 06z/18z).
Data is slower — usually available 6–9 hours after initialization.Converted to Hong Kong Time:
ECMWF Run (Initialization)	Approx. Data Available (HKT)
00:00 UTC	08:00 – 12:00 HKT
12:00 UTC	20:00 – 00:00 HKT (next day)
06:00 UTC (supplementary)	14:00 – 18:00 HKT
18:00 UTC (supplementary)	02:00 – 06:00 HKT (next day)

Quick Daily Routine for You in Hong Kong
Morning (around 8–11 HKT): Check new 00z GFS + ECMWF
Afternoon (around 2–5 PM HKT): Check new 06z GFS
Evening (around 8–11 PM HKT): Check new 12z GFS + ECMWF
